# Code of Conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss proposals on their merits, assume good intent in ambiguous situations, and make feedback specific and actionable. Welcome people with different levels of experience and respond constructively to corrections.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, repeated personal attacks, and posting another person's private information without permission are unacceptable in project discussions, issues, and pull requests.

Report conduct concerns confidentially to a maintainer through a contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/chuck852). If one is unavailable, ask how to contact a maintainer privately and keep the details private. Maintainers may moderate content or restrict participation based on the severity and recurrence of the behavior.
